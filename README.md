## 🧬 About Me

I am a **Ph.D. Researcher in Artificial Intelligence at Jeonbuk National University, South Korea**, working at the intersection of **Generative AI, Foundation Models, Deep Learning, Bioinformatics, and Scientific AI**.

My research focuses on developing novel AI architectures and computational frameworks for complex scientific problems, particularly **protein–peptide interaction modeling, generative peptide design, multimodal biological learning, environmental AI, materials informatics, and scientific prediction**.

I have experience conducting end-to-end AI research — from **dataset construction and representation learning to model development, GPU-accelerated training, benchmarking, interpretation, and peer-reviewed publication**.
